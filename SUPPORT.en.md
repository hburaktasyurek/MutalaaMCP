# Support

> **Authoritative version:** This is an English translation. The authoritative Turkish policy is [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## Reporting a problem

Report reproducible, non-sensitive problems in MutalaaMCP through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hburaktasyurek/MutalaaMCP/issues): documented
source-checkout installation, local HTTP/OAuth or `stdio` startup, the documented configuration
contract, or a defect in the client's handling of a supported upstream response.
Include the source revision, operating system, affected client name and version,
sanitized reproduction steps, and the expected and observed result.

Do not include access tokens, refresh tokens, server secrets, personal data,
legal queries, document text, or other sensitive material in a public issue. Use
the private process in [`SECURITY.en.md`](SECURITY.en.md) for security-sensitive
reports.

## Support boundaries

MutalaaMCP is legal-research software, not a law firm or legal-advice service.
It does not provide legal advice, legal opinions, representation, or a
professional-client relationship. Users must assess the law and verify results
against authoritative sources for their own matters.

The project cannot guarantee the availability, completeness, accuracy, freshness,
or continued interface compatibility of official and other upstream sources. It
cannot correct an upstream record, obtain a document unavailable from its
publisher, or override an upstream rate limit, access rule, or service outage.
Report source-content errors and account or access questions to the relevant
upstream or Mütalaa service through its official support route.

## Out of scope

- Interpretation of statutes, decisions, or search results.
- Advice about a user's legal rights, deadlines, litigation, or transactions.
- Recovery of data that an upstream source does not provide.
- Requests to bypass source terms, technical safeguards, rate limits, or authentication.
- Support for modified forks unless their maintainer assumes that support.
