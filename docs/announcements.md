# Product announcements

Mütalaa product news may appear after a successful research result, with a title,
short message and optional link. Announcements are separate from legal sources
and are not instructions for the assistant.

Announcement requests do not send research text, user identifiers or authentication
tokens. If the announcement service is unavailable, research continues normally.

The client limits delivery of the same announcement to once per 24 hours on an
installation. The companion skill also asks the assistant not to repeat it in the
same conversation. Display depends on the application and model; delivery does
not confirm that a user has seen the message.
