This policy concerns the names **MutalaaMCP** and **Mütalaa**, and the
project's associated logos (together, the “Marks”). It is intentionally narrow:
it does not alter the MIT license for the code and does not grant a separate
copyright license. Nothing here states that a Mark is registered in any
jurisdiction.

## Permitted uses

You may use the Marks truthfully to identify the unmodified MutalaaMCP project,
its official distributions, or compatibility with it. Nominative uses such as
“works with MutalaaMCP” and accurate references to an unmodified package are
permitted, provided they do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, affiliation, or
origin that does not exist.

## Forks and modified distributions

The MIT license permits forks and modifications. A fork or materially modified
distribution must use a name and visual identity that do not create confusion
with the official MutalaaMCP project. It may describe itself accurately as
“based on MutalaaMCP” or “a fork of MutalaaMCP,” but must not present itself as
the official project, an official release, or an official support channel.

Do not use the Marks or logos in a domain name, package name, social profile,
application title, or promotional material when that use is likely to cause
confusion about source, endorsement, maintenance, or support.

## Not permitted

Without separate written permission, do not:

- state or imply that a modified build is official or endorsed;
- use a logo in a way that suggests affiliation or certification;
- use the Marks to market an unrelated product or service; or
- remove or obscure project-origin notices from an unmodified distribution.

may have under applicable law.
